## Design Context

### Users
Italian families who cook together. The app is used in the kitchen while preparing meals — often with busy hands, natural light, and a daily rhythm. It's not a discovery or social app: it's a personal, private tool, used by people who know it well. The context is warm, domestic, concrete.

### Brand Personality
**Curated, elegant, gastronomic.** Like a quality Italian cookbook — refined but not cold, inspirational but not pretentious. It has the solidity of a well-made object: quality paper, precise typography, natural colors.

In 3 words for the design process: **precise, warm, editorial.**

### Aesthetic Direction
**Light mode.** Warm backgrounds like cream/parchment paper, not pure white. Earthy accents: terracotta, sage green, ochre. No blue, no cold neutral gray.

**Italian typography.** The natural choice for "gastronomic + elegant + Italian" is Bodoni Moda (Google Fonts) — a revival of the Italian typographer Giambattista Bodoni, used for centuries in Italian publishing and fashion. High contrast, authority, but warmth in the curves. For the body: Jost — a contemporary humanist sans, readable at every size, not on the reflex list.

**OKLCH palette:**
- Primary background: oklch(97% 0.01 75) — warm cream, never pure white
- Main text: oklch(18% 0.03 55) — very dark brown, never pure black
- Primary accent: oklch(52% 0.13 42) — terracotta/rust (replaces the current generic red)
- Secondary accent: oklch(50% 0.08 148) — sage green
- Card surface: oklch(95% 0.01 75) — just darker than the background
- Border: oklch(88% 0.02 75) — warm light taupe

**Anti-references (explicit):**
- No Instagram/food social: no huge photos, no likes/followers
- No AI/SaaS: no cold dashboards, purple-blue gradients, neon glow
- No delivery app: no cart, prices, promotions
- No corporate: no cold corporate style

### Design Principles
1. **Cookbook over app** — every screen should feel like a page of a curated cookbook, not a software interface.
2. **Content first** — text, ingredients, steps: they are the substance. The interface serves them, it doesn't smother them.
3. **Warmth without noise** — natural colors and elegant typography create atmosphere; no decoration for its own sake.
4. **Readability in the kitchen** — large text, high contrast, generous touch targets. The user has flour-covered hands.
5. **Privacy as a feature** — no social, no forced sharing. This is your digital kitchen.
